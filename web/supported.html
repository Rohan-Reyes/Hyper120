Compatibility

Supported phones & games

Which phones Hyper120 works on, and how fast each game can go.

Support still depends on the phone itself.
  • The screen must support 90Hz or 120Hz (or more). A 60Hz screen can't go higher.
  • Settings → Display → Refresh rate must be on the highest option.
  • The game must offer a high frame rate, and many only show it on phones the developer has approved.

Compatibility reports for MIUI and HyperOS

Results depend on the phone, ROM and game. In-app tests measure the Hyper120 screen. Gameplay reports record a specific game.

Verified

Xiaomi 14 (houji), HyperOS 3 · Android 16, China ROM. 60, 90 and 120Hz confirmed with dumpsys.

In-app tests

Xiaomi 17, Global HyperOS 3, worked after selecting 120 Hz in Display settings. POCO X8 Pro, EEA HyperOS 3, measured about 121 FPS. Xiaomi 15, India HyperOS 3, measured about 90 FPS at 90 Hz.

Gameplay reports

POCO F8 Ultra, HyperOS 4, reached 120 FPS in World of Tanks Blitz. POCO F6, India HyperOS 3, ran Clash of Clans above 60 FPS.

Needs a retest

Xiaomi 18 Pro, China HyperOS 4, measured 90 FPS for a 120 Hz request. Redmi Note 15 Pro Global was reported stuck in Clash of Clans.

Won't work

Phones with a 60Hz screen, and phones that aren't Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO (no PowerKeeper).

Tried it on another phone? Report your device → In the app, a failed Apply check has a Report button that fills in the details for you.

LIMIT 1 · THE PHONE

HyperOS can cap games at 60 Hz

Apply shows the requested rate, the display mode and FPS on Hyper120’s test screen.

LIMIT 2 · THE GAME ENGINE

Some games never go past 60

Roblox, Genshin Impact and others are built to stop at 60 on Android. Nothing on the phone side can change that.

LIMIT 3 · THE GAME'S WHITELIST

120 only on approved phones

MLBB, PUBG and CODM show their 90/120 option only on phones the developer has approved. If you don't see it, the game is still the limit.
